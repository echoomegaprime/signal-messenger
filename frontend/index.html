How to send a hidden message

1 Both you and the person you are messaging must have the same passkey. Share it with them once in person, by phone, or via a method separate from the message itself.

2 You type your message into the ENCODE tab and click Encode. You get back a string of scrambled characters that looks like gibberish.

3 You paste that scrambled string into WhatsApp, SMS, email, or any message platform. Anyone who sees it cannot read it.

4 The recipient goes to whisper.echo-op.com, enters the same passkey, pastes the scrambled text into the DECODE tab, selects their language, and reads the original message.

About the passkey

The passkey is used to generate the encryption key for your messages. Without the correct passkey, the decoded output will be unreadable garbage. The passkey is never transmitted, never stored, and never leaves your device. It exists only in your memory and the memory of your recipient.

About the languages

The decoder can deliver your message in three languages. English — the original message as you wrote it. Urdu Roman — phonetic Romanized Urdu. Nepali Roman — phonetic Romanized Nepali. Words not in the dictionary are kept in English.

Privacy

The cipher tool runs entirely in your browser. No message is ever sent to any server. The Messenger tab uses end-to-end encryption via Cloudflare infrastructure. Video calls are peer-to-peer — no video passes through any server.
